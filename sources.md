# Sources

This file contains the citations for the data sources used in this project.

## Vote Count Data

*   **The Daily Star:** [https://www.thedailystar.net/election2026data](https://www.thedailystar.net/election2026data)
*   **TBS News:** [https://www.tbsnews.net/election-2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/election-2026)

## Poverty Data

*   **Poverty Map of Bangladesh 2022:** [https://wfp.tind.io/record/130676](https://wfp.tind.io/record/130676)
    *   Published by the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), World Bank, and World Food Programme (WFP) in December 2024.
    *   Title: "Poverty Map of Bangladesh 2022: Small Area Estimation — District and Upazila Results"

## Census & Population Data

*   **Population and Housing Census 2022 (Preliminary Report):**
    *   Published by the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS) in August 2022.
*   **Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX):**
    *   Population & Housing Census (BBS): [https://data.humdata.org/dataset/population-and-housing-census-dataset](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/population-and-housing-census-dataset)
    *   Subnational Population Statistics (OCHA): [https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ps-bgd](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ps-bgd)

## UNICEF Data

*   **Bangladesh MICS6 (Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey, 2019):**
    *   Published by UNICEF.

## Geospatial Data

*   **Constituency Boundaries (GRED):**
    *   Derived from the GeoReferenced Electoral Districts (GRED) dataset for Bangladesh's 2008 constituency boundaries.
